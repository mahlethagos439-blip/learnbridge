LEARNBRIDGE ANDROID APP - SETUP STEPS
======================================

What this is
------------
A real Android app that opens LearnBridge (www/index.html) and schedules
Study Planner reminders as phone notifications. Notifications appear even
when the app is closed, play a sound, and show in the notification shade.

You need (one time, on your computer)
-------------------------------------
1. Node.js 18 or newer           https://nodejs.org
2. Android Studio                https://developer.android.com/studio
   (install it; accept the default Android SDK)
3. Your Android phone with USB debugging, or just use the APK file.

Steps
-----
1. Unzip this folder. Open a terminal inside it.

2. Install the Capacitor tools:
      npm install

3. Create the Android project (first time only):
      npx cap add android

4. Copy the latest www/index.html into the app:
      npx cap sync android

5. Open the project in Android Studio:
      npx cap open android
   Wait for Gradle to finish syncing.

6. Build the APK:
      Build > Build Bundle(s) / APK(s) > Build APK(s)
   The file appears at:
      android/app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk

7. Copy app-debug.apk to your phone and install it.
   (Allow "Install unknown apps" when Android asks.)

8. Open LearnBridge, create your account, go to Smart Study Planner,
   add a session. Tap "Allow" when Android asks for notification permission.

Important phone settings
------------------------
- Notifications: Settings > Apps > LearnBridge > Notifications > Allow.
- Sound: make sure the phone is not on Silent or Vibrate only.
- Battery: Settings > Apps > LearnBridge > Battery > Unrestricted,
  so Android does not delay reminders.
- Test: add a session 2-3 minutes from now with a 0-minute reminder,
  then close the app and wait.

Updating the app later
----------------------
Replace www/index.html with the new file, then run:
      npx cap sync android
and build the APK again.

Publishing to Google Play later needs a signed release build and a
Google Play developer account. We can do that when you are ready.

Notes
-----
- The app works offline after install. Quizzes and lessons are all in
  the file. Accounts and progress are stored on the phone only.
- The reminder sound is the phone's default notification sound.
